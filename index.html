What We Do

One Partner for Golf Simulation & Automation

NEAGLE GOLF is a global provider of tour-grade golf simulators, launch monitors, and 4K golf simulation systems for indoor golf studios, commercial driving ranges, clubs, and private homes. Our simulation packages combine high-speed camera and radar tracking with photoreal course rendering, delivering shot data accurate enough for professional fitting and training.

Beyond simulation, we engineer automated golf ball dispensers and self-service range hardware that reduce labor costs and unlock new revenue for facility operators. Every system ships with white-glove onboarding, training, and 24/7 concierge support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best golf simulator for a commercial range?

A ceiling or overhead launch-monitor system with multi-surface tracking, 4K projection, and durable enclosure — exactly what NEAGLE GOLF deploys for clubs and venues worldwide.

Do you supply automated ball dispensers?

Yes. Our dispensing machines support cashless, self-service operation and integrate with range management software for 24/7 commercial use.

Where do you ship?

NEAGLE GOLF serves clubs, ranges, and home studios worldwide, with dedicated concierge support in every region.

ABOUT NEAGLE LAB

Engineering Physical Authenticity Indoors.

Formed by a collective of aerospace radar developers, acoustic research physicists, and competitive touring professionals, NEAGLE GOLF targets the absolute apex of indoor simulation science. We do not manufacture projection toys; we craft sub-millimetric golf installations that translate true outdoor ball aerodynamics into micro-level indoor metrics.

99.9% Trajectory Precision

Every single micro-Doppler radar array undergoes stringent testing utilizing custom pneumatic launchers, performing over 100,000 strikes to map dynamic spin vectors across extreme launch windows.

Cloud-Connected Longevity

All NEAGLE frameworks support seamless over-the-air firmware upgrades. Our high-performance processing hardware guarantees continuous algorithms, features, and new LiDAR course releases.

The NEAGLE Concierge Service

From custom framing and structural sound isolation to adaptive air-flow design, our team handles all installation steps to guarantee a flawless finished sporting asset.
